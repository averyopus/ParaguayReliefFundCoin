About the project

The project’s long-term aspiration is to explore peer-to-peer use, if community adoption, technology, and applicable laws allow. This is a vision—not a promise of acceptance, utility, or value. No merchants, partners, or liquidity backers are represented here as confirmed.

Community support

We’re exploring the possibility of donating part of the project’s funds to Fundación Paraguaya and are looking into other organizations as well. This is not yet a confirmed donation commitment or partnership. We’ll publish details if a donation plan is established.

This is an independent meme-token project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Paraguay, Banco Central del Paraguay (BCP), any bank, public fund, or financial institution. It is not government-backed, legal tender, or a claim on oil or public funds.
